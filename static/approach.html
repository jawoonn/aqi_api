Built on real observations

Trained to notice
the invisible.

Photo Air was trained with around 7,000 sky images collected in California and Seoul over six months. Each photo was matched with a nearby PM2.5 sensor reading, helping the model learn the relationship between what the sky looks like and the particles in the air.

A helpful estimate, not a replacement. Image-based results can make local air awareness more accessible, but official monitoring remains the best source for health decisions.
